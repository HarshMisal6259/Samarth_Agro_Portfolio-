About Samarth Agro

Rooted in Agriculture. Driven by Quality.

Established in 2020, Samarth Agro is an agricultural processing business based in Koor, Bhudargad, Kolhapur, Maharashtra.

Our factory focuses on processed cashew nuts and bulk supply for business customers. With dedicated processing machinery and a focus on product quality, we aim to build reliable supply relationships with packaging factories and other businesses.

From our village in Kolhapur, we connect agricultural processing with business opportunities across Maharashtra.
